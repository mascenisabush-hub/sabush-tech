FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do I need a POS?

No. Sabush BPT works independently of any POS system. It's built for capital and profitability management — not for processing sales at the counter.

Can I use existing inventory?

Yes. Start by recording your initial stock count, and Sabush BPT uses it as the starting point for your capital.

Can I import products?

Yes, products can be imported to save time during initial setup.

How is profit calculated?

Profit is calculated per stock batch, based on cost versus selling price — so you see the real embedded profit in every unit of stock.

How often should I perform stock verification?

We recommend periodic verification — for example, monthly or quarterly — to confirm your records match physical reality.
